Audio .wav

The full mix with your volumes, mutes, reverb, echo, drive and tone. 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo.

MIDI .mid

Every note, exactly as placed, one track per instrument with tempo and velocity. Drums land on channel 10.

Files arrive inside a .zip, because this page can only hand out zip files. Open it and the .wav or .mid is inside.

Roblox doesn't let audio leave the game, so the WAV is rebuilt here. Synth sounds (supersaw, pads, plucks, synth basses) use the same patches as the game. Recorded instruments like piano, guitar and the drum kits are imitated, so they sound close but not identical. For a custom audio ID, load the MIDI into a music program and pick your own instruments.
